Inventory Management Dashboard | Tableau

Interactive dashboard to analyze inventory performance, wastage trends, supplier lead time, and business investment distribution.

Project Overview

This dashboard analyzes inventory wastage trends, stock movement, reorder requirements, and supplier lead-time performance to help businesses improve operational efficiency and reduce inventory loss.

The analysis identifies products with excess stock, low inventory, and high wastage risk while highlighting supplier performance bottlenecks and procurement inefficiencies.

Project Details
  • Tools: Tableau, Excel, Data Visualization
  • Domain: Supply Chain Analytics
  • Focus Areas: Inventory Optimization, Wastage Analysis, Supplier Performance
  • Dashboard Type: Interactive Business Intelligence Dashboard

Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

Average Supplier Lead Time

Monitor supplier efficiency and delivery delays.

Total Suppliers

Track supplier base growth and vendor relationships.

Total Inventory Value

Monitor the overall financial value of stock inventory.

Monthly Product Stock Count

Track monthly inventory movement and stock volume changes.

Weekly Expiring Stock Quantity

Monitor products approaching expiration dates weekly.

Weekly Expiring Inventory Value

Estimate financial risk from expiring inventory stock.

Here in this dashboard, I have showed the wastage cost growth over year, Products which have high stock, low stock, need to reorder to meet customer demand, showed the suppliers lead time of top products.

Analysis Highlights

  • Detected USD 235M+ in damaged inventory through inventory status classification and wastage analysis, helping identify major areas of financial loss and inventory inefficiency.
  • Uncovered accelerating wastage growth trends by comparing inventory growth against wastage cost and wastage percentage, enabling early risk identification and cost-control opportunities.
  • Analyzed high-value inventory products using trend visualizations and comparative charts to support better stock prioritization and investment decisions.
  • Built product dominance analysis models with scatter plot visualizations to identify products contributing most to inventory imbalance and operational bottlenecks.
  • Evaluated supplier performance efficiency by benchmarking supplier lead times against company averages, helping improve procurement planning and supply chain responsiveness.
  • Created regional investment distribution insights to assess inventory allocation balance across states and support smarter resource distribution strategies.
  • Identified the top 10 wastage-generating products to support inventory optimization, reduce operational losses, and improve overall profitability.
